📖 Game Guide & Shortcuts

• Crosshair Highlight: Selecting a cell highlights its row, column, 3×3 subgrid, and duplicate numbers.

• Candidate Notes: Toggle Notes: ON (or press N) to place 1–9 notes in any cell's 3×3 mini-grid.

• Question Cells: Starting clues are immutable and protected with a default arrow cursor.

• Controls: Use the on-screen 3×3 phone pad or your keyboard arrow keys.
